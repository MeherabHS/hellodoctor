🦟 Fever & Infections Asked 15m ago • Anonymous
Is 100.4°F fever with body pain normal on day 2 of viral fever?
"I have mild shivering and headache. Should I take antibiotics or wait?"
SA
Dr. Sabrina Akter ● BMDC Verified

Do not start antibiotics for acute viral fever. Continue strict oral hydration with ORS and plain paracetamol 500mg (max 4 times daily). If fever persists past day 3 or you notice severe abdominal pain, perform an NS1 antigen test for dengue.

🩸 Diet & Diabetes Asked 1h ago • Anonymous
Fasting blood glucose is 6.8 mmol/L. Can diet alone bring it below 6?
"I was diagnosed with prediabetes last week. Looking for practical Bangladeshi food modifications."
MR
Dr. Mahbubur Rahman ● Endocrinology

Yes, 6.8 mmol/L represents impaired fasting glucose. Replace 50% of white rice with non-starchy local vegetables (potol, dharosh, lau) and lal atta ruti. Brisk walk 30 mins daily. Re-check fasting sugar and HbA1c in 60 days.

Consult Specialist →
👶 Pediatric Care Asked 3h ago • Anonymous
Baby has mild dry cough at night without fever, what soothing measures are safe?
TH
Dr. Tanvir Hossain ● Family Medicine

For children over 1 year, half a teaspoon of natural honey with warm water or tulsi leaves soothes the airway effectively. Keep the room humidified and avoid direct air conditioner draft. Consult if breathing becomes rapid or labored.
