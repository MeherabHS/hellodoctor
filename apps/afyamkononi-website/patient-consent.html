AfyaMkononi is the Kenyan patient and doctor mobile application operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform name. Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provides agreed technology and operational services under its partnership with GHI.

This Patient Telemedicine and Data Consent (“Consent Agreement”) is a dedicated informed medical consent document. It ensures that patients (and parents or legal guardians acting on behalf of dependent minors) fully understand the nature, scope, clinical benefits, and technical limitations of remote telemedicine consultations, and authorizes the processing of sensitive medical information strictly necessary to deliver digital healthcare services on AfyaMkononi.

Informed Consent Requirement Under Kenyan Law

Under the Kenya Health Act, 2017, the Digital Health Act, 2023, and the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, informed consent must be given freely, prior to medical consultation, without bundled coercion. This document is presented clearly prior to consultation booking.

1. What Telemedicine Means on AfyaMkononi

Telemedicine on the AfyaMkononi application enables you to interact remotely with a licensed, independent healthcare professional (such as a general medical practitioner or specialist doctor) without travelling to a physical clinic. Consultations occur through secure digital tools:

  • Secure, encrypted real-time clinical chat messaging;
  • Encrypted exchange of clinical documents, photographs, and laboratory test results;
  • Transmission of electronic prescriptions (Rx) and consultation discharge summaries;
  • Real-time live video consultation sessions where enabled in the mobile application.

2. Nature & Clinical Limitations of Remote Care

You understand and acknowledge that telemedicine has distinct clinical limitations compared to an in-person physical consultation:

  • No Hands-on Physical Examination: The treating clinician cannot physically auscultate your lungs or heart, palpate your abdomen, examine your inner ear with an otoscope, or measure biometric vitals directly;
  • Technological Dependences: Image quality, video resolution, camera angles, lighting conditions, or internet lag may affect the clinician’s visual assessment;
  • Incomplete Information Risks: Diagnoses and medical advice depend heavily on the accuracy and completeness of the history and symptoms you convey;
  • Potential Need for In-Person Transition: If the doctor determines that your condition cannot be evaluated safely via telemedicine, the doctor has a professional obligation to advise immediate in-person evaluation, laboratory testing, or hospital referral.

3. Patient Responsibilities & Truthful Disclosure

To ensure patient safety, you agree to:

  • Provide a complete, truthful, and accurate medical history, including existing chronic diseases, past surgeries, and allergies;
  • Disclose all current prescription drugs, over-the-counter medications, and herbal supplements you are consuming;
  • Ask clarifying questions if you do not fully understand medical instructions or prescription dosages;
  • Seek immediate in-person emergency care if your symptoms worsen suddenly;
  • Never share your AfyaMkononi account credentials or allow third parties to consult under your identity.

4. Consent to Clinical Communications

You give explicit consent to communicate with your assigned healthcare professional through authorized AfyaMkononi application channels (in-app chat, secure document transfer, and live video when activated). You agree that clinical advice delivered through these channels constitutes formal professional medical communication.

5. Consent to Health Data Processing

Pursuant to Section 44 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Digital Health Act, 2023, you explicitly authorize AfyaMkononi to collect, store, and process your sensitive health data strictly for delivering telemedicine care:

  • Your stated medical complaints, symptom timelines, and consultation reasons;
  • Consultation messages, medical summaries, and clinical advice;
  • Diagnostic laboratory reports, radiology images, and medical photographs you provide;
  • Electronic prescriptions and medication plans authored by your doctor;
  • Supporting operational timestamps necessary to ensure clinical continuity.

6. Consultation History & Care Continuity

You understand and agree that your consultation records, chat history, clinical summaries, and prescriptions are retained within your patient account profile. This ensures clinical care continuity, enables you to access prescriptions across consultations, and maintains an authoritative medical record required by Kenyan medical standards.

7. Temporary Consultation Trace (5-Day Rule)

AfyaMkononi records an ephemeral technical trace of operational events during each consultation (e.g., room entry, message delivery acknowledgement, document opening).

This temporary trace is automatically deleted after five (5) days, unless preserved under an active grievance, security investigation, or statutory legal hold. This 5-day rule applies exclusively to the technical trace; your underlying clinical consultation records and prescriptions remain preserved in your medical profile.

8. Who Can See Your Information

You understand who can see your information:

  • The doctor you book: your name, the reason for consultation you give, the documents you upload for that consultation, and your messages and video call with them;
  • That doctor, for continuity of care: from 15 minutes before your consultation until 24 hours after it ends, a summary of your earlier AfyaMkononi consultations, including those with other doctors (date, doctor and specialty, presenting problem, clinical notes, prescription and documents, but not their messages). Every view is recorded in an audit log;
  • Other doctors: nothing, unless you book a consultation with them;
  • Peer Navigators you ask for support: only your first name and initial, an age range and what you write to them;
  • Doctors answering anonymous health questions: the question only, never your name or account.

The full list, including administrators and service providers, is in the Privacy Policy.

9. Administrative Access & Quality Oversight

Authorized AfyaMkononi administrative personnel may access consultation records only under defined governance protocols: investigating formal clinical complaints, investigating fraud or security incidents, and complying with statutory court orders. All access events are strictly logged and audited.

10. Uploaded Documents & Prescriptions

You acknowledge that files and images you upload become an integral component of your permanent consultation record. Treating doctors evaluate these files under clinical discretion. Prescriptions issued are digital medical orders authored solely by the treating doctor.

11. Video Consultations & Recording Policy

Where live video consultations are active, encrypted real-time audio/video streams are established through licensed communications infrastructure (such as Agora).

AfyaMkononi does NOT record, archive, or listen to audio or video consultation sessions. If any future clinical recording feature is introduced, it will require separate, explicit, mandatory affirmative consent.

12. Email & App Notifications

You consent to receive email and in-app notifications regarding sign-in codes, consultation booking confirmations and reminders, and doctor arrival alerts. AfyaMkononi does not transmit sensitive clinical summaries or confidential diagnoses via unencrypted plain email.

13. Technology Risks & Network Interruptions

You acknowledge that telemedicine inherently relies on telecommunications networks, mobile operating systems, and electrical infrastructure. Risks include temporary connection loss, delayed messages, and video pixelation. If a technical disconnection occurs during a consultation, the platform provides automated reconnection paths.

14. Emergency Care Limitations

Emergency Acknowledgement

You explicitly acknowledge that AfyaMkononi is NOT equipped, staffed, or authorized as an emergency medical response service. For acute, urgent, or life-threatening symptoms, you agree to immediately present to the nearest hospital casualty department or contact local emergency medical services.

15. Withdrawal of Consent & Consequences

You have the legal right under the Kenya Data Protection Act to withdraw this consent at any time by contacting info@rm-consultants.asia or requesting account deletion. You acknowledge that:

  • Withdrawal prevents AfyaMkononi from providing future telemedicine consultations;
  • Withdrawal does not invalidate lawful processing and care delivered prior to withdrawal;
  • Withdrawal does not require the immediate destruction of historical medical records that clinicians and healthcare facilities are legally mandated to retain under Kenyan healthcare laws.

16. Dependents, Minors & Guardianship

Where this consent is accepted by a parent or legal guardian on behalf of a minor or dependent, the guardian solemnly confirms their lawful legal authority to authorize medical care and data processing for the minor under the laws of Kenya.

17. Patient Confirmation & Acceptance Record

Prior to your first consultation, you will be prompted to provide an affirmative, un-coerced confirmation by checking the dedicated consent checkbox in the mobile application.

Exact Patient Attestation Declaration
“I confirm that I have read, understood, and agreed to this Patient Telemedicine and Data Consent. I understand the nature, risks, and limitations of remote healthcare consultations. I hereby consent to receive telemedicine services through AfyaMkononi, operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform, and authorize the collection and processing of health information necessary to provide those services, subject to applicable Kenyan law.”
