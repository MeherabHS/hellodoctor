AfyaMkononi is the Kenyan patient and doctor mobile application operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform name. Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provides agreed technology and operational services under its partnership with GHI.

We take the protection of personal data and confidential health information with paramount seriousness. This Privacy Policy outlines what personal data we collect, why we collect it, the legal grounds upon which it is processed, who may receive or access it, how it is safeguarded, the retention schedules applied across different classes of information, and how users may exercise their legal rights under the Data Protection Act, 2019 of the Republic of Kenya, the Health Act, 2017, the Digital Health Act, 2023, and applicable Google Play Store developer requirements.

2. Technology & Platform Partner: Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) (Agreed technology, platform architecture, data, and operational partner under the RMCL–GHI partnership)
3. Digital Health Platform Brand: AfyaMkononi.app (Platform and commercial brand name; not an incorporated company or independent owner)
4. Mobile Application Brand: AfyaMkononi (Kenyan patient and doctor mobile application brand; not a separate company)

Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provides agreed technology and operational services under its partnership with GHI.”
Registered Kenyan Address: Nairobi, Kenya

Data Controller & Processor Allocation: Global Health Initiatives (GHI) acts as the primary Data Controller under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 for the telehealth operations, clinical service delivery, healthcare compliance, and regulatory governance to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC). Research and Management Consultants Ltd. 2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal and health data across all touchpoints of the AfyaMkononi ecosystem:

  • The AfyaMkononi Patient Android Application;
  • The AfyaMkononi Care Android Application;
  • The AfyaMkononi.app Doctor Web Portal;
  • The AfyaMkononi.app Administrative Operations Portal;
  • The AfyaMkononi.app Public Website (https://afyamkononi.app);
  • All patient and clinician onboarding, identity verification, telemedicine consultations, support correspondence, and grievance resolution workflows in Kenya.

Note: External third-party services linked from or integrated into our platform (such as external telecom networks) operate under their respective privacy policies.

How we handle Peer Navigators’ own data is set out in the Peer Navigator Privacy Notice.

3. Personal Data We Collect

To deliver secure, reliable digital health and telemedicine services, AfyaMkononi collects distinct categories of personal data. We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimisation, collecting only information necessary for authorized healthcare and platform purposes.

3.1 Account and Identity Information

When registering an account as a Patient, Doctor or Peer Navigator, we collect: full name, email address (verified with a one-time code), mobile telephone number, unique account identifier, role designation (Patient, Doctor, Peer Navigator, Guardian, Administrator), date of birth (where required for clinical safety or legal capacity), biological sex/gender (only where clinically or operationally necessary), and optional profile photograph.

3.2 Patient Health and Clinical Data

In the course of booking consultations and interacting with healthcare professionals, we process sensitive health information voluntarily provided by the patient or generated by the treating clinician:

  • Medical complaint, primary symptoms, and reason for consultation;
  • Medical history, allergies, existing chronic conditions, and current medications voluntarily shared;
  • Uploaded clinical documents, lab reports, diagnostic images, and referral letters;
  • Clinical consultation messages exchanged between patient and treating doctor;
  • Electronic prescriptions (Rx), clinical consultation summaries, and medical instructions generated by the clinician;
  • Appointment scheduling information, follow-up recommendations, and consultation status.

AfyaMkononi does not collect universal or unsolicited medical dossiers; clinical data processing is strictly tethered to the consultation relationship established by the user.

3.3 Doctor Professional and Licensing Information

For healthcare practitioners applying to practice on the platform, we collect: full professional name, professional registration and license number, licensing authority information (such as Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council - KMPDC), medical specialty, clinical qualifications, years of experience, submitted practicing certificates, hospital/clinic affiliations, and account verification status.

Verification Notice: Professional credential verification is conducted through a structured internal verification process by AfyaMkononi administrative and medical compliance personnel against official documentation and published statutory registers prior to practitioner activation. AfyaMkononi does not claim automated real-time statutory database integration.

3.4 Consultation Metadata and Clinical Communication

During telemedicine sessions, the system records operational consultation metadata: appointment timestamps, consultation modality (chat or video when operational), session duration, message delivery timestamps, attachment access logs, prescription download timestamps, and read/acknowledgement indicators.

Technical acknowledgement timestamps confirm solely that a message or document was transmitted and displayed on the recipient device; they do not infer clinical comprehension.

3.5 Temporary Consultation Trace Data (5-Day Rule)

To monitor real-time system performance, prevent communication failures, and resolve connection errors, AfyaMkononi creates a temporary structured consultation trace containing ephemeral operational events: consultation opened, chat room joined, message delivery accepted/rejected, notification dispatch event, document access attempt, prescription view event, and session closure.

Important Distinction Regarding the 5-Day Temporary Trace

1. Default Lifetime: The temporary operational trace is automatically purged after five (5) calendar days, unless preserved under a documented grievance investigation, security incident review, or statutory legal hold.

2. No Raw Chat Duplication: Raw clinical chat bodies, medical attachments, and prescription texts are never duplicated into this temporary operational trace.

3. Scope Limitation: The 5-day expiration applies strictly to the ephemeral operational telemetry trace. It does not mean or cause the underlying clinical record, consultation summary, chat history, or prescription to be deleted after 5 days.

3.6 Technical, Device & Security Telemetry

When accessing AfyaMkononi services, our infrastructure logs technical telemetry necessary for platform security, fraud prevention, and rate-limiting: client IP address, session identifiers (never the session tokens themselves), device operating system and version, application build version, request timestamps, and security audit records (such as failed authentication attempts). We do not collect continuous background GPS coordinates.

3.7 Support Correspondence & Grievance Records

If you contact our support desk or file a clinical grievance, we retain: complaint descriptions, submitted screenshots, support correspondence, ticket status, administrative findings, and resolution records to ensure quality of care and compliance with statutory recordkeeping obligations.

4. How We Collect Your Information

Personal and clinical data are collected through legitimate, transparent channels:

  • Directly from you: When you register an account, complete your medical profile, book a doctor, send messages, or upload documents;
  • From healthcare professionals: When a treating doctor creates consultation summaries, issues prescriptions, or updates clinical notes;
  • Automatically via service operations: System telemetry, session management, and rate-limiting records created as you use the apps;
  • From communication partners: Delivery status receipts from email and notification services.

Zero Commercial Sale: AfyaMkononi never sells, rents, monetizes, or trades patient data or health information to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial sponsors under any circumstances.

6. Special Category Health Data & Patient Consent

Health data is classified as Sensitive Personal Data under Section 44 of the Kenya Data Protection Act and is subject to stringent confidentiality obligations under the Kenya Health Act, 2017 and Digital Health Act, 2023.

AfyaMkononi processes patient health data exclusively for authorized medical consultation and platform-continuity purposes based on your explicit, informed consent obtained prior to receiving care. Patients execute our dedicated Patient Telemedicine and Data Consent during onboarding.

Note: While consent is the primary basis for telemedicine care, other lawful grounds (such as statutory legal holds, public health mandates, or medical defense against malpractice complaints) may apply to specific retention requirements as authorized by law.

What patients see about doctors

Patients see a doctor’s name, profile photo, KMPDC licence number and licensing authority, specialty, years of experience, current hospital, qualifications, verification status and whether they are on duty. Doctors’ email addresses and phone numbers are never shown to patients, and AfyaMkononi does not publish ratings or reviews of doctors.

What patients see about Peer Navigators

Patients see a Peer Navigator’s name, county, organisation, profile text and available times, never their email address or phone number. How we handle Peer Navigators’ own data is set out in the Peer Navigator Privacy Notice.

8. Administrative Access & Audit Controls

AfyaMkononi administrative personnel do not have unrestricted or unmonitored access to patient records. Administrative access to consultation data is limited to authorized compliance, clinical governance, and security personnel, and is permitted only under predefined operational circumstances:

  • Investigating a formal patient or doctor grievance;
  • Investigating suspected security compromise or fraud;
  • Manual verification of doctor credentials and medical council registrations;
  • Executing verified account-deletion requests;
  • Complying with binding statutory or regulatory orders.

Every administrative access event is attributable to an individually authenticated administrator account and recorded in immutable, timestamped security audit logs.

9. Third-Party Service Providers & Sub-Processors

We partner with enterprise technology vendors to deliver platform infrastructure. Each vendor is subject to data processing agreements enforcing confidentiality and security safeguards:

Microsoft Azure Cloud: Enterprise cloud hosting, encrypted storage, and virtual server infrastructure.
Communication Services: Transactional email and push notification services for sign-in codes, password resets and service notifications.
Real-Time Media Providers (e.g. Agora - Conditional): Where live video consultations are enabled in the mobile application, encrypted real-time audio/video communication sessions are routed through licensed media infrastructure. (Note: Audio/video calls are not recorded by AfyaMkononi).

10. International Data Transfers & Safeguards

Certain cloud infrastructure, database servers, and communication processors may operate in secure data centres located outside the Republic of Kenya.

In compliance with Part VI of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, any cross-border transfer of personal or health data is executed only under verified safeguards: processing contracts containing standard contractual clauses, proof of appropriate technical and organizational safeguards by the recipient entity, and adherence to Kenyan health data sovereignty standards.

11. Data Security Controls & Safeguards

AfyaMkononi implements comprehensive administrative, physical, and technical controls to safeguard personal and health data:

  • Transport Security: All client-server communication is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS) with cryptographic cipher suites;
  • Authentication & Session Tokens: Secure token-based session handling with short-lived access credentials;
  • Granular Authorization: Application-level and database-level row access controls ensuring data isolation between practitioners and patients;
  • Secure Object Storage: Clinical attachments and images stored in private, access-controlled cloud repositories accessible only through signed, time-limited URLs;
  • Encryption at Rest: Database volumes and file storage protected with industry-standard AES-256 encryption;
  • Audit Logging & Anomaly Detection: Centralized security event monitoring, rate-limiting against automated abuse, and defense-in-depth perimeter filters.

While we enforce rigorous security controls, no internet-based service can warrant absolute invulnerability. Users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and mobile devices.

12. Communications & Transport Protection (No False E2EE Claim)

We believe in transparent, accurate technical disclosures:

AfyaMkononi protects all consultation messages, clinical uploads, and telemedicine records in transit using encrypted transport protocols (TLS/HTTPS). Stored communications are protected by strict access control mechanisms, encryption at rest, and audit logging on AfyaMkononi cloud servers.

Clarity on Encryption Architecture: AfyaMkononi does not claim full end-to-end encryption (E2EE) where only endpoint devices hold cryptographic keys. Because AfyaMkononi must lawfully preserve clinical consultation records, generate authoritative electronic prescriptions, enable patient access to medical history across devices, and permit administrative review during formal medical grievances, consultation messages are stored securely within the managed application infrastructure.

13. Data Retention Principles

Different classes of data are retained for distinct durations based on clinical necessity, contractual commitments, statutory requirements, and security governance:

  • Temporary Operational Trace: 5 calendar days by default;
  • Account Profile Information: Maintained for the active lifetime of the account plus statutory post-closure compliance;
  • Clinical Consultation Records & Prescriptions: Retained in accordance with Kenyan medical record retention standards and legal defense periods;

For complete retention schedules and legal hold criteria, please consult our dedicated Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

14. Account & Personal Data Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and Section 40 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, Global Health Initiatives (GHI) and Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provide clear, accessible pathways for all users of the AfyaMkononi Patient and Care applications to request account deletion and erasure of their personal data:

  • Email Pathway: Write to info@rm-consultants.asia from the email address registered on your account;
  • Public Web Pathway: Visit our dedicated, publicly accessible Account Deletion Page to submit a verified deletion request using your registered email address and mobile number, without needing access to the app.

Certain clinical consultation records and prescription histories must be retained following account deletion where mandated by Kenyan medical recordkeeping laws (KMPDC standards) or active grievance holds. Retained records are restricted under strict administrative controls and are never used for commercial or operational marketing purposes.

15. Your Data Subject Rights Under Kenyan Law

Pursuant to Part IV of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, data subjects enjoy recognized legal rights regarding their personal data:

1. Right to Be InformedTo be informed of the use to which personal data is put and the identity of processors.
2. Right of AccessTo access personal data and clinical history in custody of AfyaMkononi.
3. Right to Correction / RectificationTo request correction of false, misleading, incomplete, or outdated personal data.
4. Right to Deletion / ErasureTo request deletion of data that is no longer authorized or lawfully necessary.
5. Right to ObjectTo object to processing of all or part of your personal data on legitimate grounds.
6. Right to RestrictionTo request restriction of processing under specified statutory circumstances.
7. Right to Data PortabilityTo receive personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
8. Right to Withdraw ConsentTo withdraw consent where processing is founded strictly on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to info@rm-consultants.asia. Requests are verified and processed in accordance with statutory response timelines.

16. Children & Dependent Profiles

Telemedicine consultations for minors (individuals under the age of 18) may only be scheduled and conducted through authorized Dependent Profiles created and managed by a verified parent or legal guardian.

AfyaMkononi does not permit unrestricted, unverified self-registration of minor accounts without parental or guardian oversight. The guardian confirms legal authority to act on behalf of the minor and authorizes the medical consultation.

17. Cookies & Platform Session Telemetry

The AfyaMkononi.app web portals (Doctor and Admin) use essential session cookies and local storage strictly required for:

  • Authentication and secure account session persistence;
  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection;
  • User language selection preferences;
  • Security defense-in-depth and rate limiting.

We do not deploy third-party commercial advertising trackers or cross-site behavioral retargeting cookies.

The public website sets no cookies; see the Cookie Policy.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological enhancements, operational changes, regulatory guidance from the ODPC, or evolving legal requirements.

Material revisions will be announced via in-app notifications, email announcements, or prominent website alerts prior to becoming effective. Updated versions will display an incremented version number and updated effective date. Continued use of AfyaMkononi following notice constitutes acknowledgement of the updated terms.
