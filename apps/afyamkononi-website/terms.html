6. Patient and Doctor Roles

AfyaMkononi accommodates three distinct user categories:

  • Patients: Individuals seeking remote medical consultations, clinical advice, second opinions, or medical management from licensed professionals;
  • Doctors / Clinicians: Independent healthcare practitioners verified and authorized to conduct remote consultations in compliance with our Doctor Terms and Professional Declaration.
  • Peer Navigators: Trained people who offer non-clinical peer support through the AfyaMkononi Care app after approval, under the Peer Navigator Terms they accept in the app and the Peer Navigator Privacy Notice.

7. Telemedicine Service Scope

Telemedicine involves the remote delivery of healthcare services using digital telecommunications technology. Telemedicine consultations are subject to inherent clinical limitations, including the inability to perform hands-on physical examinations, palpation, or emergency resuscitation. Treating healthcare professionals retain absolute clinical discretion to determine whether a patient’s clinical condition is suitable for remote care or requires in-person examination or hospital referral.

8. Platform Role vs. Healthcare Professional Responsibility

AfyaMkononi is a technology provider, not a medical clinic or hospital:

  • AfyaMkononi does not practice medicine, nursing, or pharmacy;
  • AfyaMkononi does not supervise, dictate, or interfere with clinicians’ clinical judgment;
  • The clinician-patient treatment relationship is established directly between the patient and the treating doctor;
  • The treating clinician is exclusively responsible for all clinical decisions, medical advice, diagnostic evaluations, referrals, and prescriptions issued during consultations.

11. Consultation Booking & Scheduling

Consultations are booked through the application based on doctor availability schedules. AfyaMkononi does not guarantee that a specific doctor will remain continuously available at all times.

12. Chat & Video Consultation Availability

Consultations may be conducted through asynchronous or real-time clinical messaging, document exchange, and—where enabled in the mobile application—live video communication sessions. Real-time video capabilities depend on reliable device hardware, camera permissions, and network connectivity. AfyaMkononi does not record consultation audio or video streams unless a specific clinical archiving feature is introduced with mandatory explicit consent.

13. Electronic Prescriptions & Clinical Records

Prescriptions generated through AfyaMkononi represent professional clinical orders authored solely by the treating doctor. Doctors are bound by Kenya Pharmacy and Poisons Board (PPB) guidelines and must not prescribe scheduled narcotics, controlled substances, or restricted medications without lawful justification. AfyaMkononi does not dispense pharmaceutical medications directly.

14. User-Provided Information & Accuracy

Patients are responsible for providing truthful, complete, and accurate descriptions of symptoms, current medications, drug allergies, and past medical history. Providing misleading or omitted clinical information severely jeopardizes telemedicine safety, and AfyaMkononi and treating clinicians disclaim liability for adverse outcomes resulting from fabricated or withheld patient history.

15. Uploaded Documents & Images

Users may upload medical records, diagnostic test results, and clinical photographs. By uploading, you warrant that you hold lawful rights to the documents. Uploaded clinical files become part of the official consultation record and are preserved in accordance with healthcare record retention obligations.
