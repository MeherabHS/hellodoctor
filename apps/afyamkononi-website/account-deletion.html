In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (Section 40 - Right to Erasure), AfyaMkononi provide all users with transparent, accessible pathways to permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

Operating & Publishing Structure Notice:

AfyaMkononi is the Kenyan patient and doctor mobile application operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform name. Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provides agreed technology and operational services under its partnership with GHI.

You may request deletion of your account at any time, whether you use the AfyaMkononi Patient app or the AfyaMkononi Care app, through this publicly accessible web page or by email, without needing access to the app.

2. How to Request Account Deletion

You can ask us to delete your AfyaMkononi Patient or Care app account in either of these ways, without needing access to the app:

  • Web form (this page): complete the deletion request form below. It prepares an email to our Data Protection Office for you to send.
  • Email: write to info@rm-consultants.asia from the email address registered on your account, with the subject “Account Deletion Request - [Your Name / Mobile Number]”.

To protect clinical records against malicious tampering or unauthorized deletion attempts, our privacy office confirms your identity through your registered email address or mobile number before processing the request.

3. Public Web Deletion Request Form

Complete this form to submit an official account deletion request to the Global Health Initiatives (GHI) Data Protection Office. Note: We never request medical records or clinical health descriptions in this form.

The mobile number saved on your AfyaMkononi account.

4. Identity Verification & Clinical Safety

Because AfyaMkononi process confidential medical and clinical information, Global Health Initiatives (GHI) enforces strict identity verification safeguards. We never execute the deletion of a patient or doctor account solely on the basis of an unauthenticated submission without first confirming the requester’s identity through the account’s registered contact details (a reply from the registered email address or a telephone call to the registered mobile number). This prevents malicious third-party tampering with patient records and clinical histories.

5. What Happens Upon Account Deletion

When an account deletion request is verified and executed:

  • Authentication Disabled: You will be immediately logged out across all mobile (AfyaMkononi Patient / Care apps) and web sessions; login credentials and authentication tokens are permanently deactivated;
  • Profile Data Removed: Your profile photo, address, communication preferences, and non-statutory metadata are purged;
  • Communications Ceased: All email and app notifications stop;
  • Ephemeral Traces Cleared: Any pending non-clinical session data or operational cache files are purged across RMCL platform infrastructure.

6. What Information Must Be Retained

Statutory Retention Exceptions Under Kenyan Law

Under Section 40 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Kenya Health Act, 2017, the right to erasure does not apply to records that healthcare providers and institutions are legally mandated to retain. Consequently, Global Health Initiatives (GHI) and treating clinicians must retain:

  • Clinical Consultation Records & Prescriptions: Required by Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC) guidelines and Kenyan medical ethics rules for continuity of care and defense against clinical malpractice claims (mandated retention period: minimum 7 to 10 years, or up to age 21 for minors);
  • Fraud & Security Logs: Records of security violations or criminal fraud investigations;
  • Active Grievance Files: Records pertaining to unresolved patient complaints or regulatory investigations.

Retained records are archived under restricted, read-only administrative security controls managed by GHI and RMCL, and are never used for commercial or operational marketing purposes.

7. Pending Matters Precluding Immediate Deletion

Account deletion will be held temporarily in abeyance if any of the following active conditions exist:

  • An active telehealth consultation is currently underway;
  • An open, unresolved patient grievance, clinical dispute, or adverse incident is under active review;
  • A statutory court order or Kenyan regulatory preservation order mandates preservation of account records.

8. Processing Confirmation & Timeline

Public web deletion requests are acknowledged within three (3) business days of receipt, and verified deletion is completed within statutory timelines (typically within thirty (30) days as provided under the Kenya Data Protection General Regulations).
