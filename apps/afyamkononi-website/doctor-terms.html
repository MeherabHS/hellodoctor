AfyaMkononi is the Kenyan patient and doctor mobile application operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform name. Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provides agreed technology and operational services under its partnership with GHI.

These Doctor Terms and Professional Declaration (“Doctor Terms”) govern the onboarding, verification, professional conduct, clinical duties, and data protection obligations of licensed medical practitioners and healthcare professionals (“Doctor”, “Clinician”, or “Practitioner”) utilizing the AfyaMkononi Care Android Application and the AfyaMkononi.app clinician infrastructure provided in partnership with Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) in the Republic of Kenya.

Professional Governance Framework

Practitioners operating on AfyaMkononi are independent medical professionals bound by the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Act (Cap 253, Laws of Kenya), the Health Act, 2017, the Digital Health Act, 2023, the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and the ethical codes established by the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC).

1. Professional Eligibility & Practising Authority

To apply for, activate, or maintain a Doctor account on the AfyaMkononi application, you represent and warrant that:

  • You hold a recognized medical qualification from an accredited institution;
  • You are duly registered and licensed to practice medicine or your stated clinical specialty by the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC) or the statutory health professional regulatory board applicable to your cadre;
  • You hold a currently valid, unexpired Annual Practising Licence (APL) in Kenya;
  • Your medical registration is in good standing and has not been suspended, revoked, conditioned, or subjected to active disciplinary censure.

2. Registration & License Information

You agree to furnish complete, authentic, and accurate documentation during onboarding, including: full legal and professional name, KMPDC registration number, annual licence number, certified specialty credentials, curriculum vitae, and professional indemnity insurance records where applicable. You must notify AfyaMkononi within twenty-four (24) hours of any change in your licensing status.

3. KMPDC & Regulatory Verification Process

AfyaMkononi verifies practitioner credentials prior to enabling patient-facing consultations.

Notice on Verification Mechanism

Credential verification on AfyaMkononi is conducted as a structured internal compliance procedure by AfyaMkononi credentialing officers against physical documents, certified digital copies, and published statutory regulatory registers (such as KMPDC public registers). AfyaMkononi does not claim an automated, real-time statutory council API integration unless such an integration is officially gazetted and deployed.

4. Verification Status Lifecycle

A Doctor account transitions through predefined regulatory states:

PENDING:Application submitted; documents awaiting manual administrative credential review. Consultation features disabled.
VERIFIED:Credentials verified against official records; Annual Practising Licence valid. Full telemedicine consultation features active.
REJECTED:Credentials invalid, incomplete, unverified, or ineligible. Access denied.
REVOKED / SUSPENDED:Licence expired, patient grievance under investigation, regulatory sanction, or terms breach. Clinical access halted immediately.

5. Clinical Independence & Medical Responsibility

You exercise complete, uncompromised, and independent clinical judgment when conducting consultations on AfyaMkononi. AfyaMkononi does not dictate, influence, or intervene in your medical determinations, diagnostic assessments, or treatment plans. You are exclusively responsible for the clinical advice and medical care you deliver to patients.

6. Patient Confidentiality & Statutory Secrecy

You are bound by strict medical confidentiality under the Hippocratic Oath, the Kenya Health Act, 2017, the Digital Health Act, 2023, and Section 44 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. You must protect patient records and consultation messages from unauthorized disclosure at all times.

7. Patient Access Boundaries

You may access a patient’s information only for consultations booked with you: their name, the reason for consultation, the documents they upload and your messages and video session with them.

For continuity of care you may also view a summary of the patient’s earlier AfyaMkononi consultations (date, doctor and specialty, presenting problem, clinical notes, prescription and documents) from 15 minutes before your consultation with them until 24 hours after it ends. Every such view is recorded in an audit log.

Attempting to browse, search, or access records of patients outside your direct clinical treatment relationship is an actionable breach of medical ethics and these Terms, resulting in immediate account revocation and regulatory reporting.

8. What Patients and Others See About You

Patients see your name, profile photo, KMPDC licence number and licensing authority, specialty, years of experience, current hospital, qualifications, verification status and whether you are on duty. Your email address and phone number are never shown to patients, and AfyaMkononi does not publish ratings or reviews of doctors.

Authorized AfyaMkononi administrators see your account details and the documents you submit for verification, with every access recorded in audit logs, as described in the Privacy Policy.

9. Telemedicine Standard of Care & In-Person Referral

The standard of care for telemedicine consultations is identical to an in-person clinical encounter. You must assess whether remote consultation is clinically appropriate for the presenting complaint. If a patient exhibits acute red-flag symptoms, respiratory distress, unstable vitals, or conditions requiring hands-on examination, you have a professional duty to advise immediate in-person attendance at a hospital casualty department or physical healthcare facility.

10. Prescribing Responsibilities & Statutory Drug Laws

Electronic prescriptions authored through AfyaMkononi must comply with Pharmacy and Poisons Board (PPB) guidelines and Kenyan prescribing regulations:

  • You must establish a valid clinical indication and check for known contraindications and drug interactions;
  • You must not prescribe scheduled controlled substances, Class A narcotics, psychotropic drugs, or medications requiring mandatory prior laboratory confirmation unless authorized under specific clinical telemedicine guidelines;
  • You are solely responsible for dosage accuracy, route of administration, and instructions for use.

11. Consultation Records & Clinical Retention

You acknowledge that all clinical consultation messages, uploaded files, and prescriptions are recorded on the platform to maintain an authoritative, timestamped medical record. Records are retained in accordance with Kenyan healthcare documentation standards.

12. Administrative Audit & Grievance Investigation

You agree that authorized AfyaMkononi medical governance and compliance officers may review consultation logs, chat transcripts, and operational traces where a formal patient complaint, grievance, or medical malpractice allegation has been registered.

13. Schedule & Availability Accuracy

You agree to maintain your availability calendar accurately. If you are unable to fulfill an active availability block or scheduled consultation due to emergency, you must promptly update your status in the application.

14. Cancellation & Rescheduling Protocol

Repeated unnotified failure to attend scheduled consultations (“no-shows”) or habitual late cancellations degrades clinical care and constitutes grounds for availability de-prioritization or account suspension.

15. Security, Credential Protection & Data Export Bans

You must safeguard your account authentication credentials with utmost diligence. You are strictly prohibited from sharing your login credentials with colleagues, clinical assistants, or third parties. Exporting, screen-recording, or exfiltrating patient health data outside authorized clinical care channels is strictly forbidden.

16. Document Storage & Personal Device Restrictions

You must not download or store unencrypted patient clinical photographs, lab reports, or consultation histories onto personal, unsecured mobile devices, public laptops, or unencrypted external drives.

17. Doctor-Authored Articles & Educational Content

Practitioners who contribute educational medical articles to the AfyaMkononi platform warrant that the content is evidence-based, medically sound, and contains zero patient identifiable information. AfyaMkononi reserves the right to unpublish misleading or unscientific submissions.

18. Prohibited Professional Conduct

Practitioners must not:

  • Misrepresent professional qualifications, board certifications, or practicing licence numbers;
  • Delegate consultations or allow unauthorized surrogates to impersonate the clinician;
  • Ask patients booked through AfyaMkononi for private payments, gifts or favours;
  • Falsify medical records, electronic prescriptions, or diagnosis summaries;
  • Engage in harassing, unbefitting, or exploitative behavior with patients.

19. Suspension & Account Revocation Grounds

AfyaMkononi reserves the right to suspend or revoke practitioner accounts immediately upon notification of KMPDC disciplinary action, expiry or suspension of annual practicing licence, substantiated patient grievance, fraud, or breach of these Terms.

20. Data Protection & Privacy Obligations

You acknowledge that your handling of patient data is subject to our Privacy Policy and Data Retention Policy. You agree to cooperate fully with AfyaMkononi’s Data Protection Officer during privacy audits.

21. Doctor Professional Declaration & Solemn Attestation

During practitioner onboarding, you must execute the mandatory solemn professional declaration by affirming the following attestation:

Solemn Practitioner Professional Declaration
“I confirm that the professional information, licensing credentials, qualifications, and registration numbers I have provided are authentic, true, and currently valid under the laws of Kenya. I affirm that I will use AfyaMkononi, operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform, strictly within the scope of my lawful professional authority, adhering at all times to clinical standards of care, patient confidentiality, statutory prescribing laws, and applicable medical codes of ethics.”
