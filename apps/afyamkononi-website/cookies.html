Cookie & Tracking Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026 | Privacy-First Architecture | Republic of Kenya

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites function securely, remember preferences, and provide operational telemetry.

Our Privacy-First Stance:Global Health Initiatives (GHI) and Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) do NOT utilize commercial third-party advertising cookies, cross-site behavioral tracking scripts, or data broker pixels across AfyaMkononi or AfyaMkononi.app.

2. Cookies and Storage We Use

This public website (afyamkononi.app)

Sets no cookies. The account deletion form stores, in your own browser only, the times you prepared a deletion request, so that repeated requests can be limited. It holds no name, number or other personal detail, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

AfyaMkononi.app web portals

The Doctor and Admin web portals use strictly necessary cookies only: the signed-in session and protection against cross-site request forgery (CSRF). Without them the portals cannot work securely.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

Most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through browser settings. You can configure your browser to reject all cookies, notify you when a cookie is issued, or delete existing cookies. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may impair form submission and platform security features.

4. Questions Regarding This Policy

If you have inquiries regarding our cookie governance or data privacy standards, please email us at info@rm-consultants.asia.
