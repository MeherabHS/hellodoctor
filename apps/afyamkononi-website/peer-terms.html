These are the terms Peer Navigators accept when they register in the AfyaMkononi Care app: the Peer Navigator Terms and the Peer Navigator Confidentiality, Conduct & Safeguarding Agreement (both version 1.0).

AfyaMkononi is operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform name. Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provides agreed technology and operational services under its partnership with GHI.

How AfyaMkononi handles Peer Navigators’ own personal data is described in the Peer Navigator Privacy Notice.

Peer Navigator Terms

1. Your role

As an AfyaMkononi Peer Navigator you offer non-clinical peer support: listening, sharing your own lived experience where you choose to, encouragement, and help finding and using health services. You are not acting as a doctor, nurse, counsellor or pharmacist. You must not diagnose, prescribe, change anyone’s treatment, interpret test results as a clinician would, or give advice that contradicts the person’s health-care provider.

2. Approval

You may support people only after an AfyaMkononi Admin approves your account. Approval can be refused, paused (suspended) or withdrawn if these terms or the Peer Navigator Confidentiality, Conduct & Safeguarding Agreement are not followed, or where needed to protect the people using the service.

3. Accurate information

Register with your real name, phone number, email and location, and keep them up to date. Do not share your account or let anyone else use it.

4. Sessions

You create your own support slots. A request appears only after a patient asks for one of your slots; you choose to accept or decline it. Accept only requests you can attend. If you cannot keep an accepted session, tell the person through the app as early as you can.

5. Using the app

Use AfyaMkononi chat and calls for peer support only. Do not move a conversation to personal phone numbers, social media or in-person meetings arranged outside the service. Do not record calls or take screenshots of conversations.

6. Referral to care

When someone needs clinical help, encourage them to book a doctor in the app or attend a health facility. In an emergency, tell them to contact emergency services or go to the nearest facility immediately.

7. No payment or gifts

Peer support through AfyaMkononi is provided without charge to patients. Do not ask for or accept money, gifts or favours from the people you support.

8. Changes

Material changes to these terms are published as a new version, which you will be asked to accept before continuing.

This is the pilot version of these terms. Before public use the operator will publish its registered legal identity and contact details.

Confidentiality, Conduct & Safeguarding Agreement

Many people who reach out to a Peer Navigator are adolescents and young people who find it hard to talk to a doctor. This agreement protects them, and you.

1. Confidentiality

Everything a person shares with you, including their HIV status, health, sexuality, relationships and family situation, is confidential. Do not share it with anyone outside AfyaMkononi, including your family, friends, community or other people you support, and do not discuss it on social media. Do not try to find out who someone is beyond what the app shows you. Confidentiality continues after a session ends and after you stop being a Peer Navigator.

2. When confidentiality must be broken

You must tell AfyaMkononi through the app’s support channel straight away, sharing only what is needed, if a person: is at immediate risk of serious harm to themselves or others; discloses that a child is being abused, neglected or exploited; or is in a medical emergency. Tell the person, where it is safe to do so, that you need to involve others to keep them safe.

3. Boundaries

Keep the relationship professional and peer-to-peer. Never start a romantic or sexual relationship with, or make sexual comments to, a person you support. Do not lend or borrow money, sell products, promote a religion or political view, or ask for personal contact details. Share your own story only when it helps the other person, and never pressure anyone to disclose.

4. Respect

Treat everyone with dignity regardless of age, gender, sexual orientation, HIV status, disability, religion or background. No judgement, shaming, threats or harassment.

5. Safeguarding young people

Be especially careful with anyone under 18. Do not ask a young person to keep secrets from the service, do not arrange to meet them, and do not contact them outside AfyaMkononi. Keep conversations within the app so they are protected and can be reviewed if a concern is raised.

6. Stay within your role

Do not give clinical advice, medicine or dosing instructions. Encourage the person to see a doctor through the app or attend a health facility, and point them to emergency services in an emergency.

7. Looking after yourself

Supporting others can be hard. If a conversation distresses you, or you feel unsafe or harassed, end the session respectfully and tell AfyaMkononi through the support channel.

8. Consequences

Breaking this agreement may lead to suspension or removal as a Peer Navigator and, where the law requires, a report to the authorities.

Contact

Questions about these terms, and safeguarding concerns that are not emergencies, can be raised through the app’s support channel or by email.
