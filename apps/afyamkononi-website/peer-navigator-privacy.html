This notice explains how AfyaMkononi handles the personal data of Peer Navigators: trained people who offer non-clinical peer support to AfyaMkononi users through the AfyaMkononi Care app.

AfyaMkononi is operated by Global Health Initiatives (GHI) under the AfyaMkononi.app platform name. Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) provides agreed technology and operational services under its partnership with GHI.

It is the same notice (version 1.0) that Peer Navigators accept when they register in the app, together with the Peer Navigator Terms and the Peer Navigator Confidentiality, Conduct & Safeguarding Agreement. How AfyaMkononi handles patients’ data is described in the main Privacy Policy.

2. What We Hold About You

We hold:

  • your full name, email address, phone number and county;
  • your optional organisation and profile text;
  • your account credentials, in protected form;
  • your approval status and Admin review notes;
  • the support slots you create;
  • the requests, messages and call records of the sessions you take part in.

3. Why We Use It

We use this information to verify and approve Peer Navigators, to let patients choose a peer and book a session, to run chat and calls, to keep the service safe, and to meet legal obligations.

Processing relies on your acceptance of the Peer Navigator Terms (contract), our legitimate interest in running a safe service, and legal obligations under Kenyan law.

4. Who Sees It

  • Patients see only your name, county, organisation, profile text and available times.
  • Admins see your account and review details, to approve and oversee peers.

Your email address and phone number are never shown to patients.

5. Information You See About Patients

Patient information, including any health information they choose to share, is sensitive personal data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

You see only a patient’s first name and initial, an age range and what they write to you. Use it only to support that person in that session, and keep it confidential as set out in the Peer Navigator Confidentiality, Conduct & Safeguarding Agreement.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information while your account is active, and afterwards only as long as required for safety, dispute, audit and legal purposes. Session records are kept on the same basis.

The Data Retention Policy describes AfyaMkononi’s retention and deletion practices in full.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to or restrict processing. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.

You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at www.odpc.go.ke.

8. Security, Service Providers & Hosting

Peer Navigator data is protected, processed by service providers and hosted in the same way as all AfyaMkononi data. See the Privacy Policy sections on service providers, international transfers and data security.

9. Contact

For questions about this notice or to exercise your rights, contact the Data Protection Office:
