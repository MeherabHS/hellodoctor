About AfyaMkononi · Republic of Kenya

Healthcare that feels closer to home.

AfyaMkononi is a Kenya-focused digital healthcare platform connecting patients with licensed physicians through a thoughtful mobile experience, designed in English and Kiswahili.

Explore patient app → Doctor network →
Core Conviction

“Good care begins when someone feels heard. The right doctor, in the language that feels natural, without bureaucratic delay.”

Built from the ground up to respect patient autonomy, clinical judgment, and the reality of Kenyan mobile connectivity.

Guiding Commitments

Built on four foundational pillars.

We deliberately avoid unsupported patient counts, fabricated wait-time promises, synthetic outcomes, or marketing hyperbole. The platform speaks honestly about what it delivers.

01

Language Dignity

Healthcare should not require translation when you are vulnerable. AfyaMkononi is engineered with native bilingual capabilities in English and Kiswahili across all screens and notifications.

02

Verified Licensure

Every practicing clinician on AfyaMkononi is verified with the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC), ensuring standard-of-care consultations and clinical safety.

03

Data Sovereignty & Privacy

Compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (ODPC) and the Digital Health Act, 2023. Patient health data is never monetized, sold, or shared without explicit, informed consent.

04

Offline Resilience

Engineered for Kenyan network conditions. The Flutter applications employ robust local SQLite cache stores, background retry queues, and graceful degraded states during intermittent mobile data connectivity.

Authoritative Governance Structure

Institutional and Technology Architecture

To maintain complete regulatory transparency under Kenyan law and Google Play developer policies, AfyaMkononi operates through a clear, multi-organization framework:

1. Telehealth Operator Global Health Initiatives (GHI)

Registered in Kenya as the primary Data Controller under the KDPA 2019. Responsible for clinical governance, healthcare compliance, practitioner onboarding, and regulatory reporting to the ODPC and KMPDC.

2. Digital Health Platform AfyaMkononi.app

The core digital healthcare technology brand, powering modular telemedicine, electronic medical records, waiting rooms, and clinical messaging across multiple healthcare ecosystems.

3. Technology Partner Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL)

Agreed engineering, platform architecture, cloud hosting, and technical security partner under the GHI–RMCL partnership agreement. Acts as Data Processor under Kenyan law.

Emergency Notice & Clinical Boundaries

AfyaMkononi is not an emergency response service.

If you or someone in your care is experiencing acute chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, major physical trauma, sudden loss of consciousness, poisoning, or active hemorrhage, do not wait for an app response. Immediately proceed to the nearest hospital casualty department or contact local emergency medical services in Kenya.
