URGENT: AFYAMKONONI SUPPORT IS NOT AN EMERGENCY SERVICE

If you, a patient, or a family member are experiencing a medical emergency, severe breathing difficulties, chest pain, stroke symptoms, major physical trauma, poisoning, severe allergic reactions, or acute hemorrhage, DO NOT WAIT FOR A SUPPORT REPLY.

You must immediately present to the nearest hospital emergency room (casualty) or contact appropriate local emergency medical response services in Kenya.

3. Technical & Application Support

The technical support team for AfyaMkononi assists with platform operational issues, including:

  • Trouble downloading or updating the Android applications from Google Play;
  • Email verification code delivery failures;
  • Consultation room connection or message synchronization failures;
  • Diagnostic image or lab document upload errors;
  • Prescription PDF download difficulties.

Contact: Email info@rm-consultants.asia from your registered email address, with your device model and a screenshot of the error.

4. Account & Profile Support

For assistance updating your registered phone number, correcting typographical errors in your legal name, adding dependent profiles, or managing communication preferences, email info@rm-consultants.asia from your registered email address.

5. Patient Grievances & Clinical Complaints

Global Health Initiatives (GHI), as the Kenyan operator of AfyaMkononi, maintains procedures for reviewing clinical and service complaints in connection with telemedicine consultations delivered through the platform. If you experience unsatisfactory service, unbefitting conduct, an unfulfilled consultation, or a clinical concern regarding a consultation with an independent practitioner:

Grievance Redressal Procedures
  1. Submit Complaint: In the app, open the consultation and tap Report Consultation Issue, or email info@rm-consultants.asia with your registered phone number and subject line “Clinical Grievance - [Consultation Details]”;
  2. Preservation of Relevant Records: Registering a formal grievance flags the consultation records and relevant session logs for retention throughout the investigation period;
  3. Clinical and Administrative Review: Inquiries involving medical conduct are examined with the treating practitioner and designated clinical governance advisors in accordance with professional standards;
  4. Resolution & Communication: You will receive written communication regarding findings, or corrective steps following completion of the internal review.

6. Doctor & Peer Navigator Support

Registered healthcare practitioners may contact the dedicated Practitioner Support Desk regarding:

  • KMPDC annual practising licence verification updates;
  • Doctor Web Portal login and session troubleshooting;
  • Availability calendar configuration and schedule blocks;
  • Peer Navigator approval status, profile and support slots.

Practitioner Desk Contact: info@rm-consultants.asia (Attn: Clinician Operations).

7. Privacy & Data Subject Rights Inquiries

To exercise your statutory rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (such as requesting copies of your records, updating medical history, or withdrawing consent), or to initiate account deletion:

  • Review our Privacy Policy for detailed rights disclosures and processing legal bases;
  • Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Web Resource to submit an account and personal data deletion request;
  • Consult our Data Retention and Deletion Policy for statutory retention schedules and clinical record retention periods;
  • Submit privacy inquiries to us at info@rm-consultants.asia.

8. Security Vulnerability & Incident Reporting

Global Health Initiatives (GHI) and Research and Management Consultants Ltd. (RMCL) welcome responsible security disclosures from cybersecurity researchers. If you identify a potential security vulnerability or suspect unauthorized data access:

Contact: info@rm-consultants.asia

Please practice responsible disclosure: do not access, alter, or download real patient health data. Security reports are reviewed by our engineering and security team.

9. Regulatory Escalation: Kenya ODPC & Medical Council

If your complaint regarding data protection or medical practitioner conduct cannot be resolved through internal review procedures, you may escalate to the relevant statutory regulatory authorities in Kenya:

Data Protection Inquiries & Complaints
Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) Kenya

Statutory oversight authority established under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. To review current statutory complaints guidance, filing procedures, and verified contact channels, please consult the official ODPC website directly:

Official ODPC Website (https://www.odpc.go.ke)
Medical Practitioner Conduct Inquiries
Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC)

Statutory regulatory body established under the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Act (Cap 253, Laws of Kenya) governing medical practitioners, dentists, and health institutions. Formal written complaints regarding clinical conduct may be submitted via:

Official Complaints Email: legal@kmpdc.go.ke
KMPDC Public Information & Complaints Portal
