Welcome to AfyaMkononi! We're happy you're here.

Your health is personal, and getting support should feel easy, safe and free from judgment.

At AfyaMkononi, we believe every young person deserves the confidence to ask questions, explore their options and make informed decisions about their health.

Here's what you can explore:

  • Connect with care: Reach qualified healthcare professionals when you need guidance.
  • Ask freely: Find information and support in a respectful, youth-friendly space.
  • Stay informed: Explore reliable health information relevant to your life.
  • Take charge: Make healthcare decisions at your own pace, with greater confidence.

No judgment. No unnecessary barriers. Just a space designed to support your health and well-being.
