Hello Grace,

Thank you for registering as an AfyaMkononi Peer Navigator.

We've received your application. Peer Navigators help young people, including those living with HIV, feel heard, supported and less alone on their health journey.

What happens next?

An AfyaMkononi Admin will review your application. We'll email you as soon as a decision is made. You don't need to apply again while your application is being reviewed.

Until then you can sign in to the AfyaMkononi Care app to check your status, but you can't accept support requests yet.

Thank you for sharing our commitment to support without stigma.
